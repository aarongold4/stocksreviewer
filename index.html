Why this exists

Primary-source fundamentals, not repackaged vendor data

Most finance sites license their numbers from data vendors whose terms forbid redistribution — so you pay, or you get a thin free tier. StocksReviewer reads the same underlying source those vendors do: the structured XBRL data every US public company files with the SEC. That data is public domain, which is why this site can show it all, for free.

Every figure traces to a 10-K or 10-Q. The ratios, growth rates, and valuation model are computed in the open and documented here. Price charts come from TradingView; everything else is filings.
