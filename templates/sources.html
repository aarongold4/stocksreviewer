Data sources

Where the data comes from

Fundamentals — SEC EDGAR (XBRL)

All financial-statement data is sourced from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission's EDGAR system via its public XBRL APIs (data.sec.gov). Since 2009, US public companies have been required to file financial statements in XBRL, a machine-readable tagging format. This data is a work of the U.S. government and is in the public domain, which is what allows StocksReviewer to publish it freely. Company identity and ticker-to-CIK mapping come from the SEC's published company tickers file.

Prices — TradingView

Interactive price charts are embedded using TradingView's free widget, which supplies its own market data under its own license. StocksReviewer does not collect, store, or redistribute price or quote data.

Refresh cadence

Fundamentals are refreshed as new annual filings are processed. The SEC's XBRL data typically updates within a minute of a filing being disseminated. Each company page shows the date its data was last extracted and links to the live EDGAR record.

Attribution

If you reuse figures from this site, cite the underlying SEC filing rather than StocksReviewer — we're a convenience layer over the primary source, and the filing is the authority.
